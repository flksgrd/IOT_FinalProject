\section{Pain -- the problem we solve}
\label{sec:pain}

This project addresses challenges related to everyday household waste management. Certain user groups, such as elderly or physically impaired individuals, may experience difficulties handling tasks like closing trash bags or regularly emptying the bin. These tasks can require physical effort, and consistent attention, which are not always feasible for all users.\\
Additionally, even for fully able users, forgetting to empty the trash or monitor the status of waste disposal can lead to practical inconveniences, hygiene issues, and unpleasant odors—especially in environments where organic waste is stored.\\
Another challenge is the lack of awareness regarding the current state of the trash bin and the availability of replacement bags. Without a clear overview, users may only react when problems arise, rather than preventing them.\\
Overall, there exists a need for solutions that can reduce physical effort, improve awareness, and support more consistent and hygienic waste management in everyday settings.

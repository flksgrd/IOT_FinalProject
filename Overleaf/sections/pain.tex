\section{Pain -- the problem we solve}
\label{sec:pain}

Anyone who has shared a kitchen knows the problem: the bin overflows because nobody noticed it was full, the bag rips when you finally try to close it, and the spare bags ran out two days ago without anyone realising. For elderly or physically impaired users the problem is worse — tying a full bag and carrying it out is genuinely hard if your grip or mobility is limited. We wanted to build something that handles the annoying part (closing the bag and keeping track of the state) so the user only has to swap in a fresh one when they get the notification.

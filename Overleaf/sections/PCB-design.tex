\subsection{PCB-design}
\label{sec:PCB design}
As there was a lot of wires to keep track of we decided to make a PCB board. The PCB schematic and board was made in KiCad. The board collects all the wiring into a single compact design, replacing the breadboard prototype and reducing the risk of loose connections. The NodeMCU footprint was manually defined , as standard KiCad libraries did not include the correct pin spacing.
